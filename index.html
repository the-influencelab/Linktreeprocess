All your links, one tap from your Instagram profile

A simple guide to setting up Linktree and adding it to your Instagram business account, so anyone visiting your profile can reach everything you offer.

Someone finds you on Instagram, likes what they see and wants to know more. They tap the link under your bio and a clean page opens with your website, your book, your newsletter and a way to book a call. No searching, no dead ends.
Part 1

Set up your Linktree

  1. Open Chrome and go to linktr.ee, then tap Sign up. The free plan is enough.
  2. Choose your username. This becomes your link, for example linktr.ee/theinfluencelab, so pick something people will recognise.
  3. You will land on the Links screen. Tap Add.
  4. Paste a web address and give it a short title, such as "Book a call". Repeat for every link you want.
  5. Drag the links into order, with the most important at the top.
  6. Open Appearance to add your logo or photo and choose colours and fonts that match your brand.
  7. Tap Share at the top right, then Copy to copy your Linktree address.

Part 2

Add it to Instagram

  1. Open the Instagram app on your phone and go to your profile.
  2. Tap Edit profile.
  3. Tap Links, which sits just below the Bio box.
  4. Tap Add external link.
  5. Paste your Linktree address into the URL box and add a title such as "All my links".
  6. Tap Done at the top right to save.

Part 3

Check it works

  1. Go back to your profile. Your Linktree link now shows just under your bio text.
  2. Tap it yourself and make sure your Linktree page opens with all your links.
  3. Ask a friend to try it from their own phone, so you know it works for visitors too.

Worth knowing

When you add or change links later, you only update Linktree. The link on Instagram stays exactly the same. Links typed into the bio text itself will not be clickable, so always use the Links field.
